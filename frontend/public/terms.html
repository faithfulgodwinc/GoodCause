GoodCause Terms of Service

Effective date: September 13, 2026

Membership

GoodCause Membership is a recurring purchase of ongoing platform features and benefits. It is not a direct charitable donation. Apple or Google processes native subscription payments, and RevenueCat manages subscription data and entitlements. Membership renews automatically until cancelled through your store settings.

Impact Commitment: GoodCause commits 80% of confirmed net membership proceeds to eligible verified causes. GoodCause retains the remaining 20% for platform development, verification, security, support and operations.

Confirmed net membership proceeds means amounts GoodCause actually receives after store commissions, taxes, refunds, chargebacks, currency conversion and payment adjustments. Allocations cannot exceed a cause's remaining goal. Amounts that cannot be distributed become restricted rollover reserved for future eligible causes.

Direct campaign donations

A direct Paystack donation is a separate one-time payment designated for the campaign selected by the donor. It is not part of GoodCause Membership or the monthly Impact Commitment allocation.

Transparency

GoodCause publishes confirmed allocation periods and labels amounts as allocated, paid, cancelled or rolled over. Earlier reports are not silently rewritten; later corrections are recorded as adjustments.

Tax status

Membership purchases and campaign payments are not represented by GoodCause as tax-deductible. Seek independent tax advice for your circumstances.

Campaign conduct

Campaign organizers must provide truthful information. Fraud, impersonation, illegal activity and misleading fundraising are prohibited. GoodCause may suspend or reject campaigns and withhold platform access while concerns are reviewed.

Child safety

GoodCause explicitly prohibits Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), grooming, trafficking and any content or behavior that harms or exploits children. GoodCause may remove content, suspend accounts and report suspected child safety violations to relevant authorities. Our public Child Safety Standards explain these rules and provide a child safety contact.
