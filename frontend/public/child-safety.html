GoodCause Child Safety Standards

Effective date: October 1, 2026 | Published by GoodCause

GoodCause operates the GoodCause mobile application and fundraising platform. These standards apply to all users, campaign organizers, donors, public campaign pages, uploaded media, messages, comments, updates, and any other user-generated content on GoodCause.

GoodCause explicitly prohibits Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), grooming, trafficking, sexualization of minors, and any content or behavior that harms or exploits children.

Zero-Tolerance Child Safety Policy

GoodCause does not allow any content, campaign, account, link, image, video, audio, message, or activity that depicts, promotes, solicits, normalizes, facilitates, threatens, or attempts Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation. This includes real, simulated, fictionalized, AI-generated, illustrated, or otherwise altered sexual content involving minors.

Prohibited Conduct

  • Uploading, sharing, requesting, selling, linking to, or distributing CSAM or CSAE-related material.
  • Sexualizing minors or describing minors in a sexual, exploitative, coercive, or predatory manner.
  • Grooming, sextortion, trafficking, coercion, or attempts to move a child to another platform for unsafe contact.
  • Using fundraising campaigns, updates, comments, images, videos, or profile information to exploit, endanger, identify, shame, or target a child.
  • Impersonating a child, guardian, school, charity, medical provider, or authority figure to exploit children or deceive donors.
  • Posting content that exposes private information about a child in a way that creates a safety risk.

Enforcement

GoodCause may remove content, suspend campaigns, disable accounts, preserve evidence, restrict payouts, and block access when child safety concerns are identified. Severe or credible child safety violations result in immediate enforcement action.

Where required or appropriate, GoodCause reports suspected CSAE or CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), law enforcement, relevant child protection authorities, payment partners, app stores, or other safety organizations.

Moderation and Review

GoodCause reviews campaign content, uploaded media, reports, and platform activity using a combination of automated checks, human review, user reports, and administrative moderation tools. Campaign approval or verification never permits a user to violate these standards.

Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Anyone can report suspected child safety issues involving GoodCause, including CSAE, CSAM, grooming, exploitation, or unsafe content involving minors.
