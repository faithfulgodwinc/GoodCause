GoodCause Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 11, 2026 | Last Updated: September 11, 2026

1. Introduction

GoodCause ("we", "our", or "us") operates the GoodCause mobile application and fundraising platform. We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of data privacy, trust, and transparency for our community of donors, organizers, and non-profit partners.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, process, and disclose your information across the GoodCause platform.

2. Information We Collect

To operate our verified fundraising platform, we collect the following personal and device data:

  • Account Credentials: Full name, email address, profile picture (via Google Sign-In, Apple Authentication, or email OTP verification).
  • Fundraising & Story Content: Campaign details, written descriptions, campaign photos uploaded from your device photo library (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES), and audio recordings added to campaign updates (RECORD_AUDIO).
  • Transaction Data: Record of donations, contribution history, and subscription entitlements. All credit card and payment processing is handled directly by PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways and RevenueCat. GoodCause does not store credit card numbers.
  • Device & Analytics Identifiers: Technical log data including device model, operating system version, app diagnostics, and crash logs to maintain app stability.
Zero Data Selling Guarantee: GoodCause does not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or contact details to third-party advertisers.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your information strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Processing donations and issuing verified donation receipts.
  • Verifying campaign creator identity to prevent fraud and maintain community safety.
  • Enabling campaign creators to post multimedia updates (photos and audio notes) to campaign supporters.
  • Providing customer support and communicating critical account notifications.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Partners

We only share data with infrastructure partners required to deliver our core services:

  • Supabase Cloud: Encrypted database and user authentication.
  • RevenueCat: Subscription management and entitlement verification.
  • Google & Apple Authentication: Secure single-sign-on verification.

5. Account Deletion & Data Rights (Google Play Compliance)

GoodCause respects user rights under GDPR, CCPA, and Google Play Store policies. You have the complete right to request the deletion of your account and personal data at any time.

How to Delete Your Account:

1. In-App Deletion: Open GoodCause → Go to Profile → Tap "Legal & Privacy" → Select "Data & Deletion" → Tap "Request Account Deletion".

2. Web / Email Request: Send an email to privacy@goodcause.app from your registered email address with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Upon request, your user profile, campaign drafts, and personal data will be deleted within 48 hours. Financial donation records are retained strictly in an anonymized format as required by accounting regulations.
